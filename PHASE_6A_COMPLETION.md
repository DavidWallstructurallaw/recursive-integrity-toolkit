## Scientific and operational limits

Observed disappearance concerns the supplied samples and declared
representation. It does not establish permanent extinction, causal effects or
model-performance failure. Supplied ancestry and roots describe declared
topology, without authenticating provenance or independent truth. A shared
retrospective graph does not reconstruct historical knowledge. Missing,
unknown, unresolved, valid zero and unavailable evidence remain distinct.

The 100-row detail cap preserves full aggregates but does not bound every
ordinary report section or diagnostic stream. Those sections still grow with
input. RSS records disclose inherited high-water floors and cannot be treated
as incremental allocation estimates. Timeouts and finite graph guards are
operational controls, with no universal wall-time/RSS SLA.
